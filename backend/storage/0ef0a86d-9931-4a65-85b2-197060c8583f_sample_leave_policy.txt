# Company Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per year. Leave must be requested at least 5 business days in advance. Unused leave can be carried forward up to 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave
All employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave annually. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Eligible employees may take up to 26 weeks of maternity leave. Paternity leave is available for up to 2 weeks following the birth or adoption of a child.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year as per the national calendar. Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive double pay or a compensatory day off.

## Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. A stable internet connection and appropriate workspace are mandatory requirements.
